The models package groups orthogonal decision-making primitives used by the trading agent: transformer-based and Dreamer policy networks (transformer_policy.py, dreamer_agent.py, dreamer_components.py), Monte Carlo tree search for planning (mcts.py), an ensemble voting layer over multiple policies (ensemble.py), meta-learning utilities (meta_learning.py), adversarial training helpers (adversarial_training.py), and a risk/positioning stack (risk_supervisor.py, position_sizing.py, ensemble.py) that sits on top of policies to gate trades. Children communicate through shared Python module imports rather than explicit contracts; there is no central registry or orchestrator in __init__.py, so consumers import individual components directly.
