The root orchestrates a linear data-to-deployment pipeline across child modules: the `data_pipeline` ingests OHLC and macro series, the `features` package aggregates them into a 140+ observation vector consumed by the `environment` module's Gymnasium envs (long-only/long-short with realistic spread/slippage/impact), which are then driven by policy/risk models in `models` via the `training` entry points (`train_ultimate_150.py`, `train_dreamer.py`) to produce checkpoints; `evaluation` reuses the same env/feature contract to backtest those checkpoints against baselines and crisis periods. Top-level scripts (`evaluate_model.py`, Colab notebooks) and docs (`COLAB_TRAINING_GUIDE.md`, `DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md`, `FREE_DEPLOYMENT.md`, `DREAMER_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE.md`) bind the stages together, while `.env.example` centralizes MetaAPI credentials shared by live-trading paths.
